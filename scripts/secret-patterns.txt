# Stripe secrets that must never be committed: a live or test secret key, a
# restricted key, or a webhook signing secret, each followed by at least 24
# letters or digits. One extended regex per line; # lines are comments. Read
# by scripts/hooks/pre-commit (all staged files) and server/test.js
# (.env.example). Short fakes such as sk_test_local stay below the bar.
(sk_live_|sk_test_|rk_live_|rk_test_|whsec_)[A-Za-z0-9]{24,}
